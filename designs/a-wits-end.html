Ten of you, one very long table

Sleeps
Up to 10 guests. The main bedrooms take eight, and every bed can be set up as singles or doubles. The cinema room has a new king size bed built in.
Bedding
Every bed gets a super king size duvet, whatever its size. Linen is at least 300 thread count, and duvets and pillows are anti-allergy.
Washing
Two toilets and one shower. The shower has Bluetooth, so you can listen to your favourite tunes.
Eating
A fully equipped kitchen, a large dining table the whole group can sit around, and a bespoke bar area.

The hot tub runs on wood, not buttons

It is wood fired and sits in a private garden, open to the air, so you can enjoy looking at the stars. The charge covers cleaning, filling and pre-heating it, the fresh water and the wood.

Sensible questions

Are there good train links?

Yes. Chester is 2 stops (30 mins) from Cuddington, and Liverpool is 4 stops (30 mins) from Hartford.

Is there parking?

Yes. There is a large car park, shared with the guests of our converted horsebox.

What is there to do nearby?

Wildshore for water activities is 4 miles, a 10 minute drive. Oulton Park for touring cars and superbikes is 6 miles, 15 minutes. Delamere Forest for Go Ape, mountain biking and walking is 8 miles, 16 minutes.

Do you have a hot tub?

Yes. It is wood fired, in a private garden, open to the air so you can enjoy looking at the stars. It is £75 for the first use and £30 for each use after.
