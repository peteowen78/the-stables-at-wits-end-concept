Come round the yard

Step into a world where rustic repurposed charm meets modern comfort. Inside the old stables you will find cosy furnishings, old timber and brick, and room for ten to spread out.

  • Living room

    Sofas for everyone, with a piano and a guitar for those musically inspired.

  • Games room

    Pool table, table football, darts, PlayStation, board games and cards.

  • Kitchen and bar

    A fully equipped kitchen, a large dining table for the whole group and a bespoke bar area.

  • Bedrooms

    Beds for eight in the main bedrooms, and a king size bed built into the glow-in-the-dark, star-studded cinema room.

Where everyone sleeps

Up to ten guests, and every bed made up the way your group needs it.

Beds
All beds in the main bedrooms, which take eight people, can be set up as singles or doubles. The cinema room has a new king size bed built in.
Bed linen
Every duvet is super king size, whatever the bed. Linen is a minimum of 300 thread count, and all duvets and pillows are anti-allergy.
Bathroom
Two toilets and one shower. The shower has Bluetooth so you can listen to your favourite tunes.

Out the back: a wood-fired hot tub

It sits in a private garden, open to the air, so you can enjoy looking at the stars.

£75for its first use
£30for each use after

The cost covers the time taken to clean, fill and pre-heat it, the fresh water and the wood to warm it. If you would like to use it, please let us know before you book.

Also on site: a 3 par golf course and laser tag.
