“Nobody wants to be the first to go to bed.”

So said one guest. We know the feeling.

The Stables at Wits End is a family-run holiday house for ten in the Cheshire countryside at Hartford. It is lovely by day. It is at its best once it gets dark.

How an evening tends to go

  1. First, light the hot tub

    It is wood fired and sits in a private garden, open to the air, so you can enjoy looking at the stars.

    £75 for its first use, then £30 for each use after.

    That covers cleaning, filling and pre-heating it, the fresh water and the wood. Please tell us before you book if you would like it.

  2. Then one long table

    A fully equipped kitchen, a large dining table for the whole group to sit around, and a bespoke bar area for afterwards.

  3. A film, under a second set of stars

    The cinema room is glow-in-the-dark and star studded, with plenty of room to lounge out and relax. Just bring your own popcorn.

  4. Then the games come out

    Pool table, table football, darts, PlayStation 2, laser tag, board games and cards. There is a piano and a guitar for those musically inspired, and a 3 par golf course for the morning after.

  5. And, eventually, bed

    Sleeps up to ten. The main bedrooms take eight, with beds set up as singles or doubles, and the cinema room has a king size bed built in. Every bed has a super king size duvet, linen of at least 300 thread count, and anti-allergy duvets and pillows.

    Two toilets and one shower, which has Bluetooth for your favourite tunes.
