A converted stable for ten in Hartford, Cheshire. Wood-fired hot tub, pool table, cinema room.

Awarded 10 out of 10 at the Booking.com Traveller Review Awards 2025, and 9.8 in 2026. A guest favourite on Airbnb, with Superhost status.

The hot tub runs on wood

Open air and wood-fired. We clean it, fill it with fresh water and heat it before you arrive, so ask us before you book.

£75 for its first use, then £30 for each use after that. Fresh water and wood included.

Brick, beams and plenty to do

Pool, table football, darts, a PlayStation, board games, a piano and a guitar. Nobody has to go out.

“Long dinners turn into late-night laughter and nobody wants to be the first to go to bed.”
From a guest review quoted on the Stables’ own website

Room for all ten

The main bedrooms sleep eight, and every bed can be set up as a single or a double. The cinema room has a king size bed built in for the other two.

Bedding

Every duvet is super king size, whatever the bed. Linen is at least 300 thread count, and duvets and pillows are anti-allergy.

Bathrooms

Two toilets and one shower. The shower has Bluetooth, so you can listen to your favourite tunes.

For when you do go out

Wildshore
Water activities. 4 miles, a 10 minute drive.
Oulton Park
Touring cars and superbikes. 6 miles, 15 minutes.
Delamere Forest
Go Ape, mountain biking and walking. 8 miles, 16 minutes.
By train
Chester is 2 stops from Cuddington. Liverpool is 4 stops from Hartford. About 30 minutes each.
